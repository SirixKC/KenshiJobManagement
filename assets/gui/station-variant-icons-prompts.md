# Station visual subtype icon prompt set

Generation mode: built-in `imagegen`, with
`station-category-icons-source.png` supplied as the visual style reference.

Every icon used this shared prompt:

> Create one 96 x 96 game UI pictogram in the attached icon sheet's simple
> retro-industrial style. Use a thick near-black outline, crisp pixel-clear
> edges, one centered object, generous even padding, no frame, and only two to
> four subject colors. Make it readable at 48 pixels. Use a perfectly flat
> `#00ff00` chroma-key background. Do not add text, gradients, shadows, texture
> noise, extra objects, or a watermark. Do not use `#00ff00` in the subject.

The nine subject prompts were:

1. Copper ore: one chunky angular dark rock with clear copper-orange mineral veins.
2. Iron ore: one chunky angular charcoal rock with clear pale steel-gray mineral veins.
3. Copper plates: three short, flat copper-orange metal plates with offset corners.
4. Iron plates: three short, flat steel-gray metal plates with offset corners.
5. Steel bars: three compact blue-gray steel ingot bars in a small stack.
6. Copper-alloy plates: three flat plates split cleanly into copper-orange and pale-steel halves.
7. Electronics: one square teal circuit board with four corner contacts and a central gear-like chip.
8. Crossbow: one simple side-view wood-and-metal crossbow with a taut pale-steel string.
9. Skeleton limbs: one side-view mechanical lower leg and foot with two round orange-accented joints.

The chroma key was removed locally. Each final transparent PNG was fitted to a
96 x 96 square and remapped to an explicit four-color palette.
